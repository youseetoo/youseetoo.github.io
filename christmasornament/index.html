    How to build

    From files to a glowing cube

    1. Print the sides

      Each side prints flat with its outer face on the bed and needs no supports. Cut-out sides: dark PLA, 0.2 mm layers. Shadow and lithophane sides: white PLA, 0.1–0.12 mm layers and 100 % infill, so no infill pattern shows in the light.

    2. Add the diffusers

      Cut-out sides need a white sheet behind the design: print the 0.2 mm sheet in white PLA or cut tracing paper to 30.8 × 30.8 mm. It lies in the pocket on the inside of the side.

    3. Click them into the cube

      Press each side into a window of an openUC2 cube from the outside. Small ridges on the inner edge hold it in place; the rim ends flush with the cube.

    4. Light it

      Put an LED tealight into the ring on the bottom lid (up to 37.8 mm across) and close the cube with the top lid. Use LED tealights only: PLA softens at about 60 °C.

    Made by openUC2 in Jena. The cube, the sides and the lids come from the openUC2 CAD library.
